// COMMUNITY

Built in the open, by people who give a damn.

http4k has always been a community-driven project. Most of the good ideas are stolen - borrowed, improved and given back by the developers who use it every day. Here's where they hang out, and how you join in.

  • 5M+ downloads / month
  • 200+ modules & integrations
  • 9 yrs in production, since 2017
  • 0 magic, ever.

Four ways to jump in.

Whether you want to ask a question, fix a typo or ship a whole new module - there's a door for you.

CHAT

#http4k on Kotlin Slack

The fastest way to get an answer - straight from the maintainers and thousands of fellow users.

The tools that keep us building.

A handful of generous vendors support the project with free access to their excellent tools. We're grateful - and happy to say so.

JetBrains - IntelliJ IDEA

An Open Source licence for the amazing IntelliJ IDE and JetBrains’ whole suite of developer productivity tools.

Tuple

Their superb pair-programming tool lets the http4k team collaborate in real time to build the library.

YourKit

Innovative, intelligent tools for monitoring and profiling Java and .NET applications - in support of open source.

Be excellent to each other. Every space we run - Slack, GitHub, events - follows our Code of Conduct. It's short, it's human, and we mean it. Everyone's welcome here.
